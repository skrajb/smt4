1Install the software

  1. Run Smartech-Typing-Tutor-Setup and follow the installer.
  2. Choose the folder you want (the default is fine) and keep Create desktop shortcut ticked.
  3. Open Smartech Typing Tutor from the desktop shortcut or the Start menu.
Windows 7 / 8 / 8.1: use the separate Win7 32-bit installer. The standard installer supports Windows 10 and 11.

2First launch: enter your name

The first time the app opens it asks for your name and an optional roll number. Your results and certificates are saved under this name automatically, so you never have to type it before a test.

3Activate your courses

Each course (English, Punjabi, Hindi) is unlocked with its own activation code from your institute. Open Activation in the sidebar.

  1. Type or paste the code for the course into its box.
  2. Press Activate. An internet connection is needed for this step only.
  3. The card turns green and shows the expiry date and days remaining.
Moving to a new computer? Click Deactivate on the old computer first, then activate on the new one. If the old computer is not available, send your institute the Device ID shown on this page.

4The Dashboard

The Dashboard is your home screen. Use the big buttons to jump into English, Punjabi or Hindi practice, see your personal stats and view today's top scorers.

5Pick a lesson

Each course starts with step-by-step lessons: home row, top row, bottom row, numbers, symbols, Shift keys and common words. Choose any lesson to begin. Below the lessons you will find the Speed Test and the Long Paragraph exercises.

6Take a practice test

The text to copy is shown in the Reference box. Type it in the Typing box below, exactly as shown including capitals and punctuation. A timer runs at the top.

When time ends (or you finish), the exercise number, your gross speed (WPM), accuracy, backspace count and Pass / Fail result are shown and saved. A Pass automatically creates a certificate.

For a quick timed drill, open Speed Test from the course page. Choose 1 Minute or 5 Minutes, then start typing: the timer begins with your first key. Press Space after each word. Speed, accuracy and word counts update live, and a result card with your WPM appears when time is up.

How speed is calculated: Gross speed (WPM) = total characters ÷ 5 ÷ minutes. One word counts as five characters. Accuracy matters as much as speed in real exams.

7Long paragraph exercises

For full exam-style passages, open Long Paragraph Practice from any course page and choose an exercise. New exercises are added by your institute and download automatically when the computer is online. They are kept on the computer so they still work offline.

8Track your progress and certificates

Open My Progress to see the tests you have taken in the last 3 days (older results are removed automatically): which exercise it was, speed, accuracy and result. Earned certificates are listed on the same page, and you can export that history to a CSV file that opens in Excel.

9Change the look

At the bottom of the sidebar, pick one of three colour themes (Ink & Gold, Silver Steel, Pearl Ivory). Your choice is remembered. Use Ctrl + + / - to make everything larger or smaller, and Ctrl + 0 to reset.

11Questions and troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
"Invalid activation code"Check the code for typing mistakes and make sure you used the code for the right course.
"Already activated on another computer"Deactivate it on the old computer, or contact your institute with your Device ID.
Course asks to activate againThe 90-day activation has ended. Ask your institute for a new code.
Activation failsCheck the internet connection. Activation is the only step that needs it.
Punjabi or Hindi letters do not appearAdd the language and keyboard layout in Windows Settings > Time & Language, then switch to it with Alt + Shift.
Leaderboard is emptyIt needs an internet connection. Your own results are always saved on the computer.
