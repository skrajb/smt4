Smartech Typing Tutor - how to add DEMO exercises (5-minute demo test)
Paste these blocks into your GitHub exercise file and replace the sample text with your own demo exercise.
Anything above the first ### line is ignored.

### Demo Punjabi
---
ਇੱਥੇ ਆਪਣਾ ਪੰਜਾਬੀ ਡੈਮੋ ਅਭਿਆਸ ਲਿਖੋ।

### Demo Hindi
---
यहाँ अपना हिंदी डेमो अभ्यास लिखें।
---

Notes:
- The word "Demo" in the ### line is what makes it a demo.
- The language word is optional: "### Demo" alone also works (the language is detected from the text).
- More than one demo per language is fine. Extra words become the button name:
  "### Demo English Banking" -> button "Demo Banking".
- Demo exercises show only on the Demo page, never in the Chunk / Long Paragraph lists.
